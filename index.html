Clipper

Clipper is a personal desktop tool that turns my own long-form videos (podcasts, interviews, lessons) into short clips.

It runs locally on my computer. It finds the best moments, cuts them, formats them vertically, and sends them as drafts to my own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API. I review, write the caption and publish each draft myself in the TikTok app. Nothing is ever posted automatically.

Clipper is not a public service: it is used only by its owner, on content they hold the rights to.
